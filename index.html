About Me

I am a Mechanical Design Engineer with over five years of experience developing production-ready products from concept through manufacturing. My expertise includes product and mechanism design, Design for Manufacturing (DFM), Design for Assembly (DFA), engineering calculations, FEA and CFD simulation, GD&T, tolerance stack-up analysis, design verification, prototype validation, and manufacturing process development.

Throughout my career, I have contributed to product architecture, mechanical system development, engineering documentation, assembly fixture design, prototype evaluation, failure analysis, and process optimization. Working closely with cross-functional teams, suppliers, and manufacturing personnel, I have successfully supported product validation, production readiness, and the introduction of new products into manufacturing.
